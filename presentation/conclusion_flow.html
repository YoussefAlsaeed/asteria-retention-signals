2 Prepare

Clean and flag (23 quality rules)

  • 7 duplicates removed → 2,400 people
  • 10 invalid records excluded → 2,390 measurable
  • 9 with no country: company-wide figures only

Time-safe join ("what was known then")

  • A value is used only after its publication date
  • Lags: 62 / 90 / 25 / 10 / 200 days, checked against real release dates
  • Annual values keep their own year and age

3 Measure and test

Measure the three targets

  • Group hires by month; lost = left before the 6- or 12-month anniversary
  • Unfinished groups are not yet observable, never counted as retained
  • Turnover = regretted exits ÷ mean of the 12 month-end headcounts
  • 95% interval on every rate; fewer than 30 people = not judged

Test: does the economy line up with leaving?

  • 15 models (3 targets × 5 signals), one row per person
  • Each country compared only with itself, plus a time trend
  • Correction for running 15 tests (Benjamini-Hochberg q)
